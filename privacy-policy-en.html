TeaBreak Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 24, 2026　　Effective date: October 24, 2026

TeaBreak (Chinese name「喝茶提醒」, "the App") is developed and provided by Beijing Aitongxue Technology Co., Ltd. ("we", "us"). We take your privacy seriously. This policy explains how the App handles — that is, does not handle — your information.

1. We collect nothing

The App has no internet access, no sign-up, and no tracking:

  • We do not collect your name, phone number, email, or any personal information;
  • We do not collect device information, location, contacts, photos, or clipboard content;
  • No third-party analytics, advertising, or push SDKs are embedded;
  • No data is transmitted to any server (the App makes no network requests).

2. Local data storage

Your tea/water logs and preferences are stored only in the App's local storage on your device, used to:

  1. Show your daily progress and last-7-days statistics;
  2. Schedule local reminder notifications at your chosen interval and within your reminder window.

This data never leaves your device. Uninstalling the App permanently and irreversibly deletes all of it.

3. Permissions

The App requests exactly one system permission:

PermissionPurposeCan be turned off?
Notifications (local)Send tea/water reminders at your chosen intervalYes, anytime in system settings. Only reminders stop; all other features keep working.

4. Children

The App collects no user information (including from children) and is safe for all ages.

5. Changes to this policy

If a future update ever changes how the App handles information, we will announce it in the App and on this page, and only proceed with your explicit consent.
